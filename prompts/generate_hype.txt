Rules:
- English only.
- Generate 3 to 6 canonical buyer search queries.
- Required aspects:
  function, persona, occasion.
- Include each required aspect exactly once when possible.
- Optional aspect values:
  constraint, compatibility, style, gift, spec, problem.
- Avoid generic queries like "best product", "good item", "cheap product", or "high quality item".
- Do not include the product title, model number, or brand name in any query.
  Rewrite specs and features as buyer needs instead.
  BAD:  "iphone 17 pro max 512gb with 48mp camera"
  GOOD: "flagship smartphone 48mp camera for photography"
- Ground every query in the product text or propositions.
- Do not create search queries for use cases that are not supported by the product evidence.
- Confidence must be from 0.60 to 1.00.
- If there is not enough product evidence for optional aspects, generate only the 3 required aspect queries.
